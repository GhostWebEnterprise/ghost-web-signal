Privacy & security

Proven cryptography. Clear claims.

Ghostly relies on established cryptographic protocols and libraries from the Signal ecosystem rather than implementing custom cryptography. Ghostly is independent and is not affiliated with Signal Messenger LLC or the Signal Foundation.

Release hygiene: always verify release signatures and checksums before installing APKs from third-party sources.
